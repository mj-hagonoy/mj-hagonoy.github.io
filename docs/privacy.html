Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 7, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how mjhagonoy.com ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the Route Optimizer service, including when accessed through a Custom GPT on ChatGPT.

2. Data We Receive

When you use the Route Optimizer API (directly or via a Custom GPT), the following types of data may be sent to our servers:

  • Addresses – Street addresses you provide for geocoding or route planning
  • Location data – Coordinates (latitude/longitude) and addresses
  • Technician and appointment information – Technician IDs, appointment locations, time windows, and related scheduling data
  • Route optimization inputs – Shipments, vehicles, and other inputs used to optimize routes

3. How We Use Your Data

We use this data solely to:

  • Convert addresses to coordinates (geocoding)
  • Recommend technicians for new clients
  • Optimize routes and generate directions

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or any purpose other than providing the Route Optimizer service.

4. Third-Party Services

To provide these features, we send relevant data to:

  • Google Geocoding API – To convert addresses to coordinates
  • Google Route Optimization API (Cloud Optimization) – To optimize routes and compute recommendations

These services process data according to their respective privacy policies. We do not share your data with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

We do not store your input data or results. Processing is performed on demand, and data is not retained after the response is returned.

6. Your Rights (GDPR – EU/UK)

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, you have the right to:

  • Access – Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification – Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure – Request deletion of your data
  • Restriction – Request that we limit how we use your data
  • Data portability – Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object – Object to processing based on legitimate interests

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Your Rights (CCPA – California / US)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know – Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect
  • Delete – Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out – Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination – Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Use via Custom GPT / ChatGPT

When you use the Route Optimizer through a Custom GPT on ChatGPT, relevant parts of your conversation (including addresses, technician info, and similar inputs) are sent from OpenAI to our API. OpenAI's handling of your data is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy. We process only what is necessary to provide the service.
